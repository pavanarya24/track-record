03 / Drawdown

February is not a footnote.

Before March 2026 there was no intraday stop-loss rule. Losses could accumulate past any daily threshold, and they did. The deepest drawdown in either account happened in that window.

−$24,306Combined February net P&L
−$34,128Account B max realized drawdown · 25 Feb
−$25,237Account B worst single day · 4 Feb
−$4,984Account A worst single day · 25 Feb

What changed. A 2% daily loss limit and a three-losing-day handoff rule were adopted after February. What it did not fix. Days losing more than 2% of equity still occurred after March — about four in Account A and nine in Account B. The rule reduced neither the need for monitoring nor the importance of disclosure.

05 / Verification

Check the numbers. Then discount them.

Supporting evidence can be reviewed without publishing client identifiers. The figures on this page should be read with the limitations below.

  • 01Trade history — full history for both accounts, masked CSVs.
  • 02Live review — the same history synced in TickerScribe, available as a guided review.
  • 03Statements — broker statements requestable from the account holders.

Methodology. Realized cash flow per position, net of fees, reconciled to raw account cash flows. Equity curves start at $30,000 and exclude deposits and withdrawals.

Limitations. Two accounts, one five-to-eight-month window, realized P&L only. Past results do not guarantee future results.
