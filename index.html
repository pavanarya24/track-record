04 / DRAWDOWN DISCLOSURE

February changed
the risk process.

The weakest month for both accounts is presented directly, alongside the daily losses and the subsequent rule changes.

February was a material drawdown event.

Before March 2026, there was no intraday stop-loss rule. Account B recorded the deepest drawdown in either account. After February, a 2% daily loss limit and a three-losing-day handoff rule were adopted.

01 / BEFORE

No intraday stop-loss

February occurred before an intraday stop-loss rule was in place. Losses could accumulate beyond a predefined daily threshold.

02 / CHANGE

Daily loss limit

A 2% daily loss limit was adopted after February, alongside a three-losing-day handoff rule.

03 / AFTER

Risk still needs review

Days losing more than 2% of equity still occurred after March: about four in Account A and nine in Account B. The rules reduced neither the need for monitoring nor the importance of disclosure.

06 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Research tools.
Not just trade logs.

Self-built tooling for scanning, trade review, reconciliation and strategy validation.

SC

Real-time scanner

Multi-stock WebSocket scanner using lead-lag correlation signals.

OF

Order-flow terminal

Python trading terminal with volume delta and footprint analytics.

RP

Trade reporting

Parser that turns Fidelity trade confirmations into client reports, with FIFO lot-matching P&L attribution.

MC

Research & validation

Walk-forward and Monte Carlo validation. On Nasdaq opening-range trades, range size, direction and VWAP slope held up under robustness testing. Execution overlays did not show a confirmed edge and that branch was frozen.

Tools and working practice

Daily P&L is reconciled in TickerScribe. Research is evaluated before deployment; failed ideas are frozen rather than traded. GitHub profile ↗
